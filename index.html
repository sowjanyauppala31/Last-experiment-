High Blood Pressure (Hypertension)

High blood pressure, also known as hypertension, is a condition where the force of blood against the artery walls is too high.

Causes:

  • Genetics
  • Poor diet
  • Lack of exercise
  • Stress

Symptoms:

  • Often no symptoms
  • Headaches
  • Dizziness
  • Nosebleeds

Prevention:

  • Eat a healthy diet
  • Exercise regularly
  • Manage stress
  • Monitor blood pressure
